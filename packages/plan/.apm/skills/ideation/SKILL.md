---
name: ideation
description: Refine a rough idea through a non-technical interview before feeding it into grill-me. Use when the user has an initial idea they want to develop.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Interview me about this idea until it's sharp: what it is, what it's for, how it works from the outside, and what the first version must and must not include. Help me refine it: point out gaps and contradictions, and suggest sharper or simpler versions. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time.

Stay non-technical: no stack, architecture or implementation. If I raise something technical, park it for grill-me and move on.

When we're done, suggest `/to-idea` to write it up.
